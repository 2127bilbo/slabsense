# Beckett Grading Services (BGS) card grading standards — verbatim capture

Source: https://beckett.com/grading-standards ("Beckett Grading Scale" chart, one tab per grade).
Captured 2026-09-15 from the Wayback Machine copy of 2025-12-10 (the live site was on a maintenance page
that day); each tab's text was captured by clicking it. Official wording; supersedes
`docs/grading-research/BGS_STANDARDS.md` wherever they differ.

Page notes: "Each card is thoroughly analyzed in four key areas and assigned an overall grade based upon the
card's individual characteristics. The chart below contains the basic criteria for each numeric grade."
"Half-Point Grades — Please note that Beckett Grading Services provides final grades in half-point increments
(i.e., 10, 9.5, 9, 8.5 etc.). Cards that are assigned a grade with a half-point increment typically share
characteristics from both the level above and the level below the actual grade given."
Black Label banner: "Classy, one-of-a-kind cards that are flawless to the naked eye, beautiful in every way with
4 perfect subgrades to back it up."

Subgrade definitions (page): SURFACE — "The surface of the trading card refers to the front and back of the card.
The surface should be free of stains, smudges, and scratches. Any surface damage, including manufacturer print
defects or ink smears, will result in a lower grade." EDGES — "The edges should be crisp and free of any fraying,
chipping, and dings. Cards with rough or damaged edges will receive a lower grade." CORNERS — "The card should be
free of dings, creases, bends, or discoloration." CENTERING — "A card with perfect centering has even borders on
all sides. If a card is off-center, it can affect the overall appearance of the card and lower its grade."

## Grade chart

**10 PRISTINE**
Centering: 50/50 all around on front. 55/45 or better on back.
Corners: Perfect to the naked eye and virtually flawless under intense scrutiny.
Edges: Perfect to the naked eye and virtually flawless under intense scrutiny.
Surface: No print spots. Flawless color, devoid of registration or focus imperfections. Devoid of scratches and metallic print lines.

**9.5 GEM MINT**
Centering: 55/45 both ways on front. 60/40 or better on back.
Corners: Virtually flawless to the naked eye with only slight imperfections allowed under magnification.
Edges: Virtually flawless to the naked eye with only specs of wear visible under magnification.
Surface: Virtually flawless to the naked eye with a few extremely minor print imperfections visible under magnification. Deep color, devoid of registration or focus imperfections. Devoid of scratches and metallic print lines.

**9 MINT**
Centering: 55/45 both ways on front. 70/30 or better on back.
Corners: Mint upon close inspection. Slight wear is allowed under normal scrutiny.
Edges: Virtually Mint to the naked eye. Unobtrusive specks of chipping on the borders are allowed.
Surface: A handful of printing specks or one minor spot. Very minor focus or color imperfections. Clean gloss with one or two tiny scratches barely noticeable to the naked eye. One faint, unobtrusive metallic print line is allowed.

**8 NEAR MINT**
Centering: 60/40 both ways or better on front. 80/20 or better on back.
Corners: Sharp to the naked eye, but slight imperfections allowed under close examination.
Edges: Relatively smooth borders. Specks of chipping visible to the naked eye are allowed.
Surface: A few minor print spots. Very minor color or focus imperfections. Solid gloss with very minor scratches detectable only upon close inspection. Or a subtle metallic print line.

**7 NEAR MINT** (the chart labels both 8 and 7 "Near Mint")
Centering: 65/35 both ways or better on front. 90/10 or better on back. Very slight diamond cutting is allowed.
Corners: Very minor wear on two or three corners is allowed.
Edges: Slight roughness, minor chipping or very minor notching is allowed.
Surface: A few noticeable print spots or minor speckling is allowed. Minor color or focus imperfections. Very minor border discoloration. A very minor wax stain on back. Solid gloss with a few minor scratches detectable upon close inspection. A few metallic print lines.

**6 EX-MT**
Centering: 70/30 both ways or better on front. 95/5 or better on back. Slight diamond cutting is allowed.
Corners: Fuzzy corners, but free of dings and fraying.
Edges: Moderate roughness, moderate chipping or minor notching is allowed.
Surface: Noticeable print spots. Minor color or focus imperfections. Minor border discoloration and color or focus imperfections. Minor wax stains or extremely subtle ink marks. Relatively solid gloss with minor scratches, but devoid of scuffing. Noticeable metallic print lines.

**5 EXCELLENT**
Centering: 75/25 both ways or better on front. 95/5 or better on back. Slight diamond cutting is allowed.
Corners: Four fuzzy corners, a touch of notching or a minor ding is allowed.
Edges: Noticeable roughness - but no layering. Very slight notching or noticeable chipping is allowed.
Surface: Noticeable print spots. Minor color or focus imperfections. Minor border discoloration. Minor wax stains or very light ink mark. Some gloss lost from surface with minor scratches, but devoid of scuffing.

**4 VG-EX**
Centering: 80/20 both ways or better on front. 100/0 or better on back. Moderate diamond cutting is allowed.
Corners: Slight notching or layering, or moderate dings are allowed.
Edges: Readily chipped or notched and/or slightly layered.
Surface: Heavy print spots. Hairline creases. Moderate color or focus imperfections. Moderate border discoloration. Moderate wax stains. Very light ink mark or tape stain. A good deal of gloss lost from surface. Very minor scuffing or an extremely subtle tear in the form of a touch of broken surface paper.

**3 VERY GOOD**
Centering: 85/15 both ways or better on front. 100/0 or better on back. Moderate diamond cutting is allowed.
Corners: Slightly rounded or noticeably notched corners with slight layering is allowed.
Edges: Heavy notching, moderate layering or heavy chipping is allowed.
Surface: Heavy print spots. Very minor creases. Noticeable color or focus imperfections. Noticeable border discoloration. Noticeable wax stains. Light ink mark or tape stain. Very little surface gloss. Minor scuffing or a very minor tear.

**2 GOOD** — the chart's "2 Good" tab shows the same text as "3 Very Good" (site bug as captured); no separate wording is published.

**1 POOR** — the chart's "1 Poor" tab shows the heading "FAIR":
Centering: 100/0 or offcut on front or back. Heavy diamond cutting is allowed.
Corners: Heavily rounded or heavily notched with noticeable layering.
Edges: Destructive chipping, notching or layering.
Surface: Severe print spots. Heavy creases. Severe color or focus imperfections. Heavy border discoloration. Severe stains. No original gloss. Heavy scuffing or a severe tear.

## Defect ladder (derived)

- **Creases:** none above 4; 4 = hairline creases; 3 = very minor creases; 1 = heavy creases.
- **Tears:** 4 = extremely subtle tear (touch of broken surface paper); 3 = very minor tear; 1 = severe tear.
- **Corners:** 10 perfect; 9.5 slight imperfections under magnification; 9 slight wear under normal scrutiny; 8 sharp to the naked eye; 7 very minor wear on 2–3 corners; 6 fuzzy, free of dings and fraying; 5 four fuzzy corners, a touch of notching or a minor ding; 4 slight notching or layering, moderate dings; 3 slightly rounded or noticeably notched; 1 heavily rounded/notched.
- **Edges:** 9 unobtrusive specks of chipping; 8 specks of chipping visible; 7 slight roughness, minor chipping, very minor notching; 6 moderate roughness/chipping, minor notching; 5 noticeable roughness, very slight notching or noticeable chipping; 4 readily chipped/notched, slightly layered; 3 heavy notching, heavy chipping; 1 destructive.
- **Surface:** 10 no print spots, no scratches/print lines; 9.5 a few extremely minor print imperfections under magnification; 9 a handful of specks or one minor spot, one or two tiny scratches, one faint print line; 8 a few minor print spots, very minor scratches; 7 a few noticeable print spots, very minor wax stain on back, a few minor scratches; 6 noticeable print spots, minor wax stains or extremely subtle ink marks, minor scratches, no scuffing; 5 some gloss lost, minor scratches, no scuffing; 4 heavy print spots, moderate wax stains, a good deal of gloss lost, very minor scuffing; 3 very little gloss, minor scuffing; 1 severe stains, heavy scuffing.
- **Combination rule:** the page does not publish a formula for deriving the final grade from the four subgrades; the widely reported "lowest subgrade + 0.5 (unless two subgrades tie at the lowest)" rule used by our engine is NOT on this page and remains unverified.
