# A. Apple guideline compliance matrix

Read-only audit, 2026-10-01, against the tree on `tag-dataset` (same `src/`, `api/`, `supabase/` as `main`). No native (`ios/`, Capacitor) project exists yet, so every "in the native app" status is judged on the web code that will be wrapped.

**Summary:** 5 Blocker, 8 Major, 4 Minor, 4 Note (21 findings; 4.8 and 5.6 pass outright, 1.2 and 4.8 are n/a).
**Blockers:** every digital good is sold through Stripe Checkout inside the app (A-01, A-02); subscription management/restore/terms are Stripe-portal based (A-03); account deletion does not delete the account (A-04); no privacy-policy link in the app and the policy omits the AI providers (A-05).
**Biggest unknowns:** 4.2 and 5.1.1 permission strings cannot be judged until the Capacitor shell exists; 2.1 phone-memory behaviour inside WKWebView is untested.
