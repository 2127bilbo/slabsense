## Every grade is an estimate

- A SlabSense grade is an **estimate** of how a card might be graded, produced by software from your photographs. It is not an official grade and it is not a certification of authenticity.
- The estimate depends on the photos: lighting, focus, glare, the angle of the card and how the card line is placed all change the result. Two photos of the same card can produce different estimates.
- Real grading companies examine the physical card under controlled conditions. **Their grade may differ, sometimes by more than a full grade.**
- Centering is measured from the lines you place in the centering tool. If the lines are off, the centering is off.
- Surface defects such as creases, scratches and print lines are the hardest to see in a photo and the most likely to be missed or over-called.

## The SlabSense slab

A SlabSense slab is a display case with an engraved label that records a SlabSense **estimate**. It is not a grade, authentication or guarantee from any grading company, and it should not be presented as one. The public cert page shows the estimate as recorded at the time of the order.
