# SlabSense Support

*Updated October 2, 2026*

## Contact

Email **support@slabsenseai.com**. We answer within two business days.

Include the card name and, for an AI Grade or a slab order, the date of the grade or the order
number from your receipt. Please do not email photos of payment cards or passwords.

## Common questions

**My grade looks wrong.** Every SlabSense grade is an estimate produced by software from your
photos. Lighting, focus and glare change the result; retake the photos on a plain background with
the card filling the frame. A professional grading company can grade the same card differently.

**I bought AI Grades and the balance did not change.** On iPhone, open the store screen and tap
Restore Purchases. On the web, refresh the page; purchases land within a minute of payment. If
the balance is still wrong, email us with the receipt and we will credit the account.

**A grade failed.** A failed AI Grade is refunded to your balance automatically. If it was not,
email us the time of the attempt.

**Cancel a plan.** On iPhone: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. On the web: the Manage button
on the store screen opens the billing portal. Unused plan grades expire at the end of the period;
pack grades never expire.

**Delete my account.** Settings › Delete Account removes your account, saved cards and photos.
See the Privacy Policy for what is kept for orders and payments.

**A slab order.** The public cert page linked from the slab's QR code shows the order status
(paid, engraved, shipped). Shipping questions: email us with the cert number.
